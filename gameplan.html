Standard Sales Agent launch guide

Target a measured Sales Agent pilot in five business days.

Follow one owner-led path to install, connect, configure, validate, and pilot Sales Agent for Dynamics 365. Do not add custom tools or production writes until the native, read-only path passes its gate.

Turnkey path

Five days, five control points

This is the shortest safe route to a bounded pilot. Product propagation can add time; a delay does not justify skipping a gate.

Day 0Lock scope

Name owners, users, environment, scenario, and evidence location.

Day 1Prove access

Confirm licenses, roles, app availability, and tenant alignment.

Days 2-3Configure native

Connect CRM, initialize Sales Chat, and limit the first data boundary.

Day 3Validate

Run known-answer, permission, and safe-failure tests.

Days 4-5Pilot and decide

Observe 3-5 sellers, score value, and choose the next move.

Owner map

One accountable owner for every decision

One person can fill more than one role, but no role can remain unnamed when configuration starts.

Business owner

Approves the seller job, pilot cohort, success threshold, and final decision.

Seller subject-matter expert

Provides known answers, runs the real workflow, and scores usefulness.

Tenant administrator

The Microsoft 365 administrator authorized to deploy integrated apps; confirms Copilot access, owns the pilot security group, and deploys Sales. Use the tenant-admin prerequisite link in the official deployment guide below if the owner is unknown.

Teams administrator

Creates the install/pin policy and confirms Teams access.

CRM and security administrator

Selects the Dynamics environment, applies roles, and enforces the data boundary.

Solution lead

Runs the checklist, captures evidence, triages failures, and protects the read-only boundary.

Execution runbook

Complete these eight steps in order

A step is complete only when its evidence exists and its “Done when” condition is true. If blocked, use the recovery path—never skip forward.

1

Assign owners and freeze the pilot scope

Owner: Business owner + solution lead + tenant administrator

30 minutes

Do

  1. Name the six roles in the owner map.
  2. The tenant administrator creates one Microsoft Entra Security group, adds the 3-5 named pilot sellers, assigns a named group owner, and records the group name and object ID.
  3. Confirm one Dynamics 365 environment and 3-5 pilot sellers in the same tenant.
  4. Approve one seller job using Account and Opportunity data.
  5. Select two Accounts and two to four Opportunities, or approved synthetic equivalents.
  6. Approve the protected location for evidence and the zero-write boundary.
  7. Before Sales Agent access, have each seller complete the approved Account-and-Opportunity job once without Sales Agent. Use one start/stop definition and record the completion time as the frozen baseline.
  8. Freeze a one-question 1-5 usefulness survey where 1 means “not useful” and 5 means “ready for this job without facilitator correction.”
  9. Accept the default validation and pilot thresholds below or record approved replacements.
Output
One signed-off launch card with owners, security group, environment, users, scenario, records, baseline method, survey, thresholds, and boundary.
Capture
Owner names; group name, object ID, owner, and members; approvals; record IDs; per-seller baseline times; survey wording; thresholds; and evidence-location pointer.
Done when
No required profile field says “Confirm at kickoff,” the security group has an owner and the exact pilot members, and the baseline and survey are frozen.
If blocked
Stop. Use synthetic records in a test environment or reschedule configuration after an owner resolves the gap.
2

Confirm licenses, tenant alignment, and CRM roles

Owner: Tenant administrator + CRM/security administrator

45 minutes

Do

  1. Confirm each pilot seller has access to Microsoft 365 Copilot for the Sales chat experience.
  2. In Microsoft 365 admin center, confirm Microsoft 365 Copilot agents are enabled and User access for agents is set to All users. Step 4 still restricts Sales Chat itself to the pilot group.
  3. Confirm the sellers and Dynamics environment are in the same Microsoft Entra tenant; guest users are not supported.
  4. Name one dedicated least-privileged pilot account. Default to the out-of-box Salesperson role; use Sales Manager only when the approved job requires it and record the reason. Admins use System Administrator or System Customizer.
  5. For custom roles, add the documented Sales Copilot User or Sales Copilot Administrator privileges without replacing the customer's base role.
  6. Name the Step 6 editor and have the Power Platform administrator assign that person the Copilot Studio Author role before any synonym or glossary edit.
  7. Record whether zero-write is technically enforced by a customer-approved read-only test role. If the pilot account keeps normal seller write privileges, label the boundary behavioral and audit-observed, not technically enforced.
Output
Entitlement and role matrix for every pilot user and administrator, including the named least-privileged pilot account and Step 6 Copilot Studio Author.
Capture
License assignment status, agent policy and User access status, tenant match, CRM role names, pilot-account identity, Copilot Studio Author identity, and technical-versus-behavioral write boundary.
Done when
Every pilot identity passes Copilot access, agent User access, same-tenant, and CRM-role checks; the least-privileged pilot account, write-control method, and Step 6 Copilot Studio Author are named.
If blocked
Assign the missing entitlement or role. After a role change, sign out and back in; allow up to 15 minutes for Teams to reflect CRM permission changes.
3

Install and pin the Sales app package

Owner: Tenant administrator + Teams administrator

45 minutes + propagation

Do

  1. Check whether Sales is already installed automatically for eligible Dynamics 365 Sales Enterprise or Premium users.
  2. For remaining users, follow Install Sales in Outlook and Microsoft 365 Copilot: Microsoft 365 admin center → Agents → All agents → Sales → Deploy → select the pilot group → Next → Next → Finish deployment → Done.
  3. Follow Install and pin Sales in Teams: Teams admin center → Teams apps → Setup policies → Manage policies → Add; name the policy, turn User pinning on, add Sales under both Installed apps and Pinned apps, then Save.
  4. On Group policy assignment, select Add, choose the Step 1 pilot group and the new setup policy, set the rank, and select Apply.
  5. Confirm the app is not blocked by an integrated-app, platform, or access policy.
  6. Have one test seller open Sales in Outlook, Teams, and Microsoft 365 Copilot.
Output
The Sales app is assigned to the pilot group on all required surfaces.
Capture
Deployment assignment, Teams policy, and one test-user visibility check per surface.
Done when
The same test seller can open Sales in Outlook and Teams and can see Sales in Microsoft 365 Copilot.
If blocked
Check app blocks and assignment scope first. Allow up to 48 hours for deployment before using the official troubleshooting path.
4

Connect Dynamics 365 and initialize Sales Chat

Owner: CRM administrator

30 minutes

Do

  1. Follow Access administrator settings: open the Sales personal app in Outlook or Teams, sign in with administrator credentials, select Sales in the left navigation (or View more apps → Sales), then select the Settings tab.
  2. If prompted, select Connect to CRM, then select the exact Dynamics 365 environment approved in Step 1.
  3. Open Settings → Environment → Access settings → Allow access to Sales Chat.
  4. Turn the feature on, choose Set access restrictions, add the pilot security group under Allow access, and select Save.
  5. Because meeting insights are on by default and store data in Dataverse, open Environment → Access settings → Allow meeting insights, turn the toggle off, and select Save. If existing users depend on it, stop and use a separate test environment; do not disable a live dependency.
  6. Open Features → Sales Chat at least once. The first load initializes Sales Agent and loads supported entities.
Output
A connected, initialized Sales Chat configuration restricted to the pilot group.
Capture
Environment confirmation, Sales Chat access scope, meeting-insights state, CRM connection status, and initialization timestamp.
Done when
The Sales Chat settings page loads its supported entities, access is limited to the approved group, meeting-insights storage is off for the proof, and the pilot seller can connect to the approved CRM.
If blocked
Recheck administrator privileges, environment selection, and CRM connection. Do not continue with an uninitialized page or a different environment.
5

Configure the minimum CRM data boundary

Owner: CRM/security admin + business owner

60 minutes

Do

  1. In Features → Sales Chat, capture the full default table list loaded during initialization.
  2. Select every table not approved for this proof, choose Remove, and confirm. Retain Account and Opportunity; at least one table must remain.
  3. Review every retained table: Sales Agent can access all columns in a configured Dynamics 365 table.
  4. Sign in to Power Platform admin center as a System Administrator. Open Manage → Environments → the approved environment → Settings → Product → Features → Dataverse search. Turn on Turn on search indexing to support Dataverse intelligence (Work IQ) in AI and agent experiences, then select Save.
  5. In Power Apps, select the approved environment, then Solutions → the approved solution → Edit → Overview → Dataverse search → Manage search index. Confirm Account and Opportunity are indexed. Removing either table from Dataverse Search makes it unavailable to Sales Agent queries.
  6. Capture storage usage from Power Platform admin center: Licensing → Capacity add-ons → Dataverse → select the environment → Chart; then Licensing → Dataverse → Environments → select the environment → Download Report under Search Index.
  7. Use the named least-privileged pilot account from Step 2 and confirm the selected sample records are visible.
  8. Test one record and one field that the pilot user must not see.
  9. Keep the proof read-only even if the seller's normal role permits business-record writes.
Output
A minimal approved table, column, record, search, and permission baseline for the proof.
Capture
Default and final table lists, retained-column review, exact Dataverse Search toggle state, Account/Opportunity index status, storage-report pointer, pilot account and role, allowed result, and denied result.
Done when
The configured-table list exactly matches the approved list, approved records resolve, excluded data stays excluded, and no prompt or test causes a business-record write.
If blocked
Reduce scope, correct the role or search configuration, or return to approved synthetic records. Never broaden access to make a test pass.
6

Add business language and summary instructions only where tests prove a gap

Owner: Named Copilot Studio Author + CRM administrator

45-90 minutes

Do

  1. Run the known-answer baseline before changing synonyms, glossary terms, or summary instructions.
  2. For synonyms, open Copilot Studio in the approved environment, then Agents → Copilot in Dynamics 365 Sales → Knowledge → SalesSpecificQnA → Synonyms → Select item → the approved table → Add Synonyms. Enter the synonyms and Description, then select Save. Add one only when seller language does not map to an approved field.
  3. For a glossary term, use Copilot Studio → Agents → Copilot in Dynamics 365 Sales → Knowledge → SalesSpecificQnA → Glossary; enter the documented term and description, then select Publish.
  4. For summaries, open the Sales personal app → Sales → Settings → Environment → Custom AI instructions → Account or Opportunity summary → ... → Edit. In an approved test CRM environment, mention only approved fields/relationships and select Publish.
  5. Summary instructions cannot be tested before saving. Preserve the prior value and use a test CRM environment; if no approved test environment exists, skip summary changes for this proof rather than editing production.
  6. Because adding synonyms can change which tables Sales Chat can access, return to Features → Sales Chat and recapture the final table list. It must still match the approved list from Step 5.
  7. Retest the failed question. Allow up to 15 minutes for glossary updates to become available.
Output
A versioned list of only the language and summary changes that improved a measured failure.
Capture
Before/after prompt, expected answer, changed term or instruction, score, rollback value, test-environment name, and recaptured Sales Chat table list.
Done when
Each retained customization fixes an observed gap without reducing correctness on previously passing tests, and the final table list still matches the approved list from Step 5.
If blocked
If the Copilot Studio Author role is missing, skip synonym and glossary edits. Restore a failed summary instruction or remove a failed term; do not accumulate speculative configuration.
7

Run the scripted validation and close every critical failure

Owner: Seller SME + solution lead + security owner

60 minutes

Do

  1. Freeze all 12 questions below, expected answers, source fields, and the named scorer before the run.
  2. Execute the twelve-case script below with the named least-privileged pilot account from Step 2.
  3. Record the answer, grounding source, score, latency observation, and failure type for each case.
  4. Repeat the two paraphrase tests and both negative tests.
  5. Recheck every passing case after a configuration repair.
Output
One prompt-by-prompt scorecard with reproducible evidence and an issue owner for each failure.
Capture
Prompt, response, expected answer, source, pass/fail, scorer, timestamp, and issue disposition.
Done when
The pre-pilot measures—grounding, correctness, consistency, least privilege, zero writes, and safe failure—pass with no critical false statement. Seller usefulness is measured in Step 8.
If blocked
Classify the failure as data, access, configuration, product, or scenario. Fix one layer, rerun all passed tests, and stop after one no-progress cycle.
8

Run the bounded pilot and make the production decision

Owner: Business owner

Two pilot sessions or five business days

Do

  1. Release the validated configuration only to the 3-5 named pilot sellers.
  2. Have each seller complete the same Account and Opportunity job at least twice.
  3. Use the Step 1 start/stop definition, frozen per-seller baseline times, and 1-5 survey to track successful completion, time saved, usefulness, corrections, access issues, and unsupported requests.
  4. Apply the default pilot gate: every seller completes two runs; at least 80% of runs finish without facilitator correction; average usefulness is at least 4 of 5; median completion time is at least 20% faster than baseline; and there are zero critical safety, access, or write failures.
  5. Review the issue log daily and preserve the read-only boundary.
  6. At the end, choose one outcome: stop, repair and rerun, evaluate an extension after native success, or plan production.
Output
A signed pilot decision with threshold results, owner, rationale, evidence, next checkpoint, and rollback path.
Capture
Seller/session count, unassisted completion rate, usefulness score, median time change, critical issues, support owner, and decision.
Done when
The accepted pilot thresholds pass or the business owner records the failed threshold and chooses stop or repair; every open issue has an owner and date.
If blocked
Pause the pilot, remove access if safety or permissions are in doubt, and return to the failed numbered step.

Twelve cases before pilot access

Decision

Choose exactly one decision

Do not treat pilot completion as production approval. The owner chooses the path that matches the evidence.

Stop

Use for any critical safety, access, write, or false-statement failure, or when expected value no longer justifies another cycle.

Repair and rerun

Use when any non-critical pre-pilot or pilot threshold misses; repair the native layer and rerun the applicable gate before proceeding.

Evaluate an extension

Use only when every native threshold passes and one proven required need remains unsupported by native capability.

Plan production

Use only when every threshold passes and no required need remains unsupported; complete production controls and obtain dated release approval.

Observed resultRequired next move
Any critical safety, access, write, or false-statement failureStop: remove pilot access and investigate before another run.

No critical failure, but any pilot threshold is below 80%, 4 of 5, or 20% fasterRepair and rerun: do not extend or plan production until the missed threshold passes.
Every pilot threshold passes and one proven required need remains unsupportedEvaluate an extension: apply separate security, cost, assignment, and regression gates.

Follow-up obligations, not decision options: rerun all 12 cases after any entity, role, schema, synonym, glossary, summary, or extension change. After an approved production release, review adoption, quality, access, incidents, and business outcomes weekly for the first month, then set the steady operating cadence.

Production boundary: this guide does not confirm deployment, customer acceptance, account-specific licensing or cost, capacity, or permission to change a tenant or CRM. Those decisions require their accountable owners and normal approval paths.
