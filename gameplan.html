Why now

A focused first candidate reduces risk

Your team has submitted sales use cases and asked Microsoft to recommend a starting point. The recommended first move is small enough to prepare before a custom build.

1

Use native capability first

Validate Sales Agent setup, CRM access, and the seller job before adding new tools or knowledge.

2

Test known answers

Use approved sample records and 10-15 questions whose answers a seller subject-matter expert already knows.

3

Decide from evidence

Measure grounding, correctness, consistency, permissions, usefulness, and safe failure before expanding.

What starts before the build

Prepare the proof, not a custom solution

These tasks establish the data, access, and evaluation boundary without committing to an extension.

Confirm the route

Identify the approved Dynamics 365 CE environment, proof users, and the customer owners for CRM, security, and seller validation.

Choose sample records

Select two Accounts and two to four Opportunities that are approved for the session, or use equivalent synthetic records.

Freeze known answers

Write 10-15 questions, expected answers, source fields, and the seller-SME scorer before running the proof.

Protect the boundary

Use the least-privileged test role, expose only approved tables and fields, and verify that no create, update, or delete occurs.

Who owns what

Role-based ownership

Confirm the people filling these roles at kickoff.

Customer business owner

Prioritizes the seller job, approves sample records, and accepts the proof scorecard.

M365 / Sales Agent administrator

Has environment-level access to Sales Agent administrator settings; installs Sales Agent in Outlook and Teams, turns on Sales Chat, opens the Sales Chat settings page once to initialize it, and confirms appropriate CRM privileges with the CRM/security owners.

CRM and security owners

Confirm environment, connection, allowed tables, privileges, Dataverse Search, and least privilege.

Seller subject-matter expert

Owns known answers, observes the run, and scores usefulness.

Microsoft account team

Keeps the engagement aligned to existing motions and customer outcomes.

Microsoft solution engineering

Designs the test, facilitates Workshop 1, captures evidence, and protects the no-write boundary.

Licensing specialist or LSP

Validates any account-specific licensing, capacity, or cost conclusion after the execution profile is known.

Readiness checklist

Confirm before executing prompts

If the checklist is not ready, Workshop 1 becomes a readiness session. No live change is required to preserve the meeting.

Sales Agent setup

  • Intended users have Microsoft 365 Copilot access.
  • The M365 / Sales Agent administrator has environment-level access to Sales Agent administrator settings.
  • Sales Agent is installed in Outlook and Teams.
  • Sales Chat is enabled in Access settings.
  • Sales Chat settings have been opened once to initialize.
  • The CRM connection is available.
  • Admin and seller CRM privileges are appropriate.

Dynamics 365 and evaluation

  • Approved tables begin with Account and Opportunity.
  • Dataverse Search and index/storage implications are understood.
  • Only needed synonyms or glossary terms are reviewed.
  • Sample records and fields are approved.
  • Known answers and the scorer are ready.
  • The test role is least-privileged and read-only.

Extension later, if justified: Sales Agent extensions copy tools and knowledge from one declarative agent as a version snapshot, require explicit user or security-group assignment, and may take up to 24 hours to appear. That is not required for Workshop 1.

Workshop 1

Native readiness and known-answer proof

A 60-minute working session with one seller job and one data boundary.

Agenda

  1. Confirm purpose and zero-write boundary - 5 minutes.
  2. Choose the seller job and sample records - 10 minutes.
  3. Review Sales Agent prerequisites - 10 minutes.
  4. Finalize questions and scoring - 10 minutes.
  5. Run Account and Opportunity prompts - 15 minutes.
  6. Test missing and conflicting data - 7 minutes.
  7. Lock the next decision and owner - 3 minutes.

Expected outputs

  • An approved seller scenario and record set.
  • A prompt-by-prompt scorecard with source notes.
  • A no-write observation.
  • An assigned list of readiness gaps.
  • A dated decision: stop, iterate native setup, or evaluate an extension.

A readiness-only session is a valid outcome when a prerequisite is incomplete.

Proof scorecard

Seven measures, one proposed gate

The final threshold is accepted by the customer business owner and Microsoft account team before it governs a decision.

Source grounding

Every factual answer identifies the relevant CRM record or source context.

Correctness

At least 10 of 12 core questions match the known answer, with no critical false statement.

Consistency

Two paired paraphrases (prompts 1/9 and 4/10) produce materially consistent answers.

Least privilege

The test user sees only approved records and fields.

Zero writes

No create, update, delete, or other business action occurs.

Seller usefulness

The seller-SME average is at least 4 out of 5.

Safe failure

Missing or conflicting data is disclosed rather than invented.

Decisions and next steps

Leave kickoff with six locks

Each lock has an owner and evidence.

1. Environment

Which Dynamics 365 CE environment and proof users are approved?

2. Scenario

Which seller job, Accounts, and Opportunities form the first proof?

3. Data boundary

Which tables, fields, and records are allowed or excluded?

4. Evaluation

Who owns known answers, scoring, and the accepted proof threshold?

5. Next checkpoint

Who closes remaining readiness gaps and by what proposed date?

6. Expansion

What result means stop, iterate native setup, evaluate an extension, or approve a pilot?

Boundaries: This plan is read-only and proposed. It does not represent deployment, production readiness, customer acceptance, account-specific pricing or capacity validation, or authorization to change CRM or tenant settings.
